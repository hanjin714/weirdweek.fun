Editorial Method

We choose topics where one odd detail opens onto a larger story about design, infrastructure, science, or public life.

What we publish

Independent, sourced explainers about inventions, design decisions, infrastructure, public experiments, and cultural curiosities. Every story follows the visible oddity back to the problem it was meant to solve.

What we look for

A strong topic has checkable source material, a real constraint or tradeoff, and a useful connection that remains interesting after the novelty wears off.

How articles are built

Each article starts with the practical question: what problem did this thing solve, what tradeoff did it reveal, and why would a curious reader care after the novelty fades?
