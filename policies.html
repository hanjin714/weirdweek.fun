Acceptable use

Enjoy the quizzes without harassing others, attempting unauthorized access, overloading the site or using results to make important decisions about someone's health, employment or finances. Results are fictional interpretations for entertainment.

Data deletion

Quiz answers are processed in page memory and disappear when the page is closed or reloaded. We currently offer no accounts or saved reports. To request deletion of an email you sent us, write to hello@weirdweek.fun; we may need to verify your request. Third-party providers control their own systems and may require a separate request.

Data retention

Quiz answers are not saved by the site. Emails remain with the email service until deleted; we have not established a fixed deletion period. GitHub and Google determine retention of their own technical and advertising data. We do not claim an unverified retention period.

Service providers and regions

GitHub Pages hosts the site; Google Fonts delivers typefaces; Google AdSense code is present for advertising. Their processing locations and use of additional providers depend on their services and settings. We have not verified all regions or contract terms. No AI model provider or payment processor is currently integrated into the quiz.

Billing and refunds

The current quiz and world results are free. There are no accounts, subscriptions, credit purchases or payments on this site. If a paid product is launched, the price, credits, refund rules and support details will be disclosed before purchase.

Cookie policy

The quiz itself does not set cookies or use local storage. Google advertising code and its partners may set or read cookies and use other identifiers, including IP addresses, for ad delivery and measurement. You can control cookies through your browser and personalized ads via Google My Ad Center. See how Google uses data. Google Fonts and GitHub may receive your IP address when resources are requested.

Cookie and advertising choices may also be offered through a consent message where configured. Contact hello@weirdweek.fun with questions.
